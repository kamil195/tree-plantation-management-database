Tree Plantation Management Database

A MySQL project for managing trees, volunteers and plantation activities while demonstrating relational design and practical SQL.

Relational design

SQL depth

Joins, aggregates, subqueries, views, trigger-based validation, transactions, savepoints and indexes.

Business questions

Track planting activity, tree height, categories, volunteer participation, recent planting and above-average trees.
